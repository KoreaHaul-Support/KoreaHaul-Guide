Naver Smart Store is not one shop. It is the storefront platform behind hundreds of thousands of independent Korean sellers, and where most of what we buy comes from.

## Why it needs Buy For Me

- Most stores require a Korean phone number to register.
- Payment is by Korean card or bank transfer.
- The address form rejects overseas addresses.

## Reading a listing

- The headline price is the cheapest option. Check the dropdown for what you actually want.
- **배송비** - domestic shipping charge. **무료배송** - free, usually above a threshold.
- **품절** - sold out. **재입고** - restocked.
- **교환·반품 불가** - no exchanges or returns.

## Official store or reseller?

Anyone can open a Naver Smart Store, so authenticity varies seller to seller. The URL tells you which you are looking at.

- [**brand.naver.com/\[brandname\]**](http://brand.naver.com/[brandname]) - the brand's own official store.
- [**smartstore.naver.com/\[anything\]**](http://smartstore.naver.com/[anything]) - an independent seller. Could be an authorised retailer, could be a reseller.

For K-beauty and anything where authenticity matters, stick to [brand.naver.com](http://brand.naver.com) or a store from our [Where to Shop](https://koreahaul.com/where-to-shop) directory.

## Other Korean marketplaces

- [**Coupang**](https://coupang.com) - fast and cheap on everyday goods, own delivery network.
    - **로켓배송** - blue or navy badge. Sold and shipped by Coupang itself, sourced from official brands. Authentic.
    - **판매자로켓** - orange badge. Shipped from Coupang's warehouse, but sold by a third party. Usually a reseller. Could be an authorised retailer, could not.
- [**Gmarket**](https://gmarket.co.kr), [**Auction**](https://auction.co.kr), [**11st**](https://11st.co.kr) - heavy overlap. Gmarket sometimes accepts foreign cards.
- [**SSG**](https://ssg.com), [**Lotte On**](https://lotteon.com) - stronger on brands and beauty, but not everything is a department store product. Both carry private sellers alongside their own stock, so check the seller before ordering.
- [**Daiso**](https://www.daisomall.co.kr/ds) - affordable stationery, household goods, and K-beauty. The online store carries the full range, but check the item is in stock before ordering.
