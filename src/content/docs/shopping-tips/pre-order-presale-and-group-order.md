Pre-orders, presales, and group orders are how most limited Korean merchandise is sold. All three work on different rules to normal shopping.

## Pre-order (예약판매)

You pay now, the store ships on a release date later. Common for albums, figures, and collaboration goods.

:::caution
The release date is when the store ships, not when it reaches us. Add Korean domestic delivery on top. Your 45 free storage days start only at check-in.
:::

- Keep pre-order items in their own request. Bundling them means the whole request waits.
- Release dates slip. The store decides, not us.
- Most pre-orders cannot be cancelled once the order is placed. Cancellation and refund terms are set by the seller, not by us.

## Presale (선판매)

Early access to a sale, before it opens to everyone. The item usually ships straight away - what is limited is who can buy and when.

- Eligibility is set by the seller, not by us. Membership, a fan club account, or a code may be required.
- Windows are short, sometimes an hour.
- We cannot guarantee we will get in. Whether a presale purchase succeeds depends on the seller's system and stock.

## Group order (공동구매)

Buyers pool together to hit a minimum quantity or unlock a bulk price. Organised on Twitter, Discord, Korean fan cafes, and [Makestar](https://www.makestar.com/).

**We welcome group orders.** Submit a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) with the details and we will contact the organiser or seller for you.

- Payment usually goes to an organiser, not a store, so there is no buyer protection.
- Contacting the organiser means seller communication, so **Standard Buy For Me** applies.
- Bulk quantities may be priced separately. Email [business@koreahaul.com](mailto:business@koreahaul.com) for wholesale volumes.

:::note[Need large quantities?]
We work with established Korean resellers who hold sold-out merchandise in bulk - light sticks for BTS, BIGBANG and other groups, often in the hundreds. If a group order needs volume we can usually source it. Email [business@koreahaul.com](mailto:business@koreahaul.com).
:::

Budget for the wait. A pre-order placed in March can reach your door in July. That is normal.
