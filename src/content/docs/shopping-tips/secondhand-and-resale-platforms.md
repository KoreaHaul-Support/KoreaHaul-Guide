KREAM, Bunjang, Joongonara, Karrot, and Soldout are where sold-out and discontinued Korean items surface. They are also where most problems start.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

These platforms are **as-is**. Once we place the order it cannot be cancelled, returned, or exchanged. We are buying from a private individual, not a retailer.

</div>

## What is different

- We do not verify authenticity and make no representation that an item is genuine.
- If a marketplace runs its own authentication, a dispute over that result goes to the marketplace, not to us.
- Damage in international transit is a shipping claim, not a return.
- Which tier applies depends on the platform and the listing:
    - **Basic ₩2,000** - KREAM and Soldout, and Bunjang or Karrot Safe Payment listings that can be bought outright.
    - **Standard ₩5,000** - Joongonara, Karrot sellers who agree to ship, and any listing where we have to negotiate or ask the seller questions.
    - **Premium ₩30,000 per unit** - Karrot listings that need in-person pickup.

## Reduce the risk

- Request extra photos before we buy. That needs Standard Buy For Me, since we have to message the seller.
- Check the seller's rating and how long the listing has been up.
- Prices well below market are a warning, not a bargain.
- For anything high-value or easily faked, buy on KREAM or Soldout. Their authentication makes them the safest option for sneakers, K-pop merchandise, and Pokémon cards.

## The platforms

- [**KREAM**](https://kream.co.kr), [**Soldout**](https://soldout.co.kr) - sneakers and streetwear, plus K-pop merchandise and Pokémon cards. Both authenticate before shipping, which makes them much safer than an open marketplace.
    - On KREAM, a **green lightning icon** means the item is already in KREAM's warehouse, authenticated and ready to ship.
    - Without it, the seller ships to KREAM first, KREAM authenticates, then it comes to us. Allow at least 5 extra business days.
- [**Bunjang**](https://bunjang.co.kr), [**Joongonara**](https://web.joongna.com/) - general second-hand, heavy on photocards and collectibles.
- [**Karrot**](https://daangn.com) - local pickup based, so listings are often a Seoul or Incheon meetup. Premium Proxy covers in-person pickup.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Box condition is not guaranteed.**

- KREAM and Soldout: packaging usually arrives in good shape, but not always. Authentication covers the item, not the box, and they do not open sealed packaging.
- Bunjang, Joongonara, Karrot: depends entirely on the seller. We have no way of knowing beforehand.
- Box condition is never grounds for a return.

</div>
