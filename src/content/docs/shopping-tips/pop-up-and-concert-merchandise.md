## How we buy it

We work with Korean purchase agents who run teams on the ground. They go to the pop-up or venue, queue, and buy on your behalf. That network is what makes this possible at all.

The ₩30,000 covers a person's time at the venue. Most or all of it goes to the agent rather than to us. It is not a markup on the item.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

**Why it is usually still worth it.** Sold-out merchandise resells well above retail in Korea. A BIGBANG light stick retails at about ₩52,000 but resells at ₩120,000 to ₩130,000.

Agents set their own price per item, so the total varies - it might come to ₩82,000 or ₩100,000. Either way it usually lands below the resale price.

We quote the full amount before you pay, so you can compare it against resale and decide.

</div>

Pre-orders for pop-up and concert merchandise go through the same route.

## What to submit

Submit a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) with:

- Pop-up or concert name, location, and dates
- Photos of exactly what you want, with item names if you have them
- Your maximum price, since this merchandise is often unpriced until the day
- Your priority order, in case items are sold out

## What we cannot promise

- **Stock.** Popular items sell out on day one, sometimes in the first hour.
- **Purchase limits.** Most pop-ups and venues cap purchases per person.
- **Queue outcomes.** Some require same-day reservation through a Korean app.

If we cannot buy the item, we refund the item price to your original payment method. The Premium fee covers the attempt, so give us alternatives up front.

## Light sticks and batteries

Most new light sticks ship without batteries. Where one is included, we may have to remove it depending on the shipping method you choose - EMS Korea Post does not accept batteries at all, and carriers that do cap it at 1 to 2 devices per shipment.

## Timing

Submit your request as early in the run as possible. The last weekend is usually picked clean, and concert merchandise often sells out before the show starts.
