Korea's biggest second-hand marketplace for K-pop, fashion, collectibles, and sold-out items.

*Last updated 8 October 2026*

[Visit Bunjang](https://m.bunjang.co.kr/) - or browse listings on [KoreaHaul's Bunjang page](https://koreahaul.com/bunjang).

## Buy For Me tier

- **Basic ₩2,000** - listings that can be bought outright
- **Standard ₩5,000** - listings where we need to message the seller, negotiate, or ask questions

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Orders are final.** Once placed, they cannot be cancelled or returned, and no refund is possible. Check the listing carefully before you request.

</div>

## Good to know

- **Digital coupon codes** need Premium Buy For Me (₩30,000 per code) and payment by Wise or Bank Transfer, because of the risk of seller fraud. Codes are sent only to your registered email.
- Items are sold by individuals, not by Bunjang. Condition and accuracy of the listing are the seller's responsibility.
- Check the photos and description carefully before you request.
- Box condition is never grounds for a return.
- More tips on the [Secondhand & Resale Platforms](/shopping-tips/secondhand-and-resale-platforms/) page.

## Shipping notes

- If the country of origin cannot be identified, the item is declared as made in China. This happens often with Bunjang items.
- **United States:** second-hand items often come without CPSC certification. Toys, plush, light sticks, and children's products may be held. See [CPSC eFiling for U.S. shipments](/news/cpsc-efiling-for-us-shipments/).
- **Branded products (Nike, LEGO, Pokémon, and similar) cannot use International Standard Shipping.**
