## Good to know

:::caution
**Weverse is not the same as Weverse Shop.** Many artists from other agencies have a Weverse community, but their merchandise is usually sold on their agency's own store - [SMTOWN & STORE](https://smtownandstore.com/) for SM artists, [YG Select](https://www.ygselect.com/) for YG artists, [FANS Shop](https://app.fans/shop) for JYP artists. Check where your artist sells before you request.
:::

## Shipping notes

- Albums, photocards, and photobooks ship without special rules.
- **Light sticks:** EMS Korea Post takes no batteries of any kind. Other carriers accept built-in batteries, 1-2 devices per shipment.
- **United States:** light sticks, plush, and dolls may need CPSC certificate information. See [CPSC eFiling for U.S. shipments](/news/cpsc-efiling-for-us-shipments/).
- Posters cost more to ship in a tube. We can pack the tube inside a regular box instead.
