[Visit Soldout](https://www.soldout.co.kr/)

## Buy For Me tier

- **Basic ₩2,000**

## Good to know

- Prices change by size. Give us the exact size in your request.
- Compare with [Kream](https://kream.co.kr/) - the same item is often listed on both, at different prices.

## Shipping notes

- Most Soldout items are branded, so International Standard Shipping is not available. Use FedEx, EMS Korea Post, or SF Express.
- Shoe boxes are bulky and add volumetric weight. Check the Shipping Calculator before you request.
