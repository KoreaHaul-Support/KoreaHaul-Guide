Daiso Korea sells stationery, kitchenware, storage, K-beauty, cosmetics tools, and seasonal goods, mostly between ₩1,000 and ₩5,000.

## Ordering

Start with the Daiso category on [**KoreaHaul Picks (KH Picks)**](https://koreahaul.com/koreahaul-shop). We have catalogued product names and links there so you can submit a Buy For Me request in a few clicks. These are curated links, not stock - we do not hold products ourselves.

The [online store](https://www.daisomall.co.kr/ds) carries the full range. Check the item is in stock before ordering - popular and seasonal lines sell out and are not always restocked.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

**No Buy For Me fee on Daiso online orders.** You pay the item price and domestic shipping only. Spend ₩30,000 or more and domestic shipping is free too.

</div>

## What is worth shipping

Daiso items are cheap but not weightless, so shipping is what decides whether something is worth it.

**Better value:**

- Stationery and planners - light and hard to find abroad
- K-beauty and cosmetic tools - Korean design well below brand prices
- Character collaborations and seasonal ranges - sell out fast
- Storage and organisers - light for their size

**Less worth it:** ceramics and glass, which are heavy and break, and anything you can buy at home for a similar price.

## Practical notes

- Put all your Daiso items in one Buy For Me request. One order means one domestic delivery.
- Daiso K-beauty has its own limit: 5 per product, per request. Other cosmetics are capped at 3.
