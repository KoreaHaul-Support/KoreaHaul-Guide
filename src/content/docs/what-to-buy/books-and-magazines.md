Korean photobooks, art books, magazines, and webtoon volumes, plus magazine issues that come with K-pop inclusions.

## Where to buy

Start with the Books category on [**KoreaHaul Picks (KH Picks)**](https://koreahaul.com/koreahaul-shop). We have catalogued product names and links there so you can submit a Buy For Me request in a few clicks. These are curated links, not stock - we do not hold products ourselves.

- [**Aladin**](https://aladin.co.kr), [**YES24**](https://yes24.com), [**Kyobo**](https://kyobobook.co.kr) - the main booksellers. Aladin also has a large used-book section, and Kyobo is Korea's oldest bookstore.

Magazine issues with idol covers sell out early. Pre-order rather than wait - pre-orders usually come with the gifts, and those run out first. See Pre-order, Presale & Group Order for how pre-orders work.

## Magazines with inclusions

Many Korean magazines ship with posters, postcards, or photocards. Listings usually say which version carries which inclusion.

## Shipping notes

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

Books are dense. One Korean magazine weighs 1 to 1.5 kg, and 1.5 to 2 kg once packed.

</div>

- Corners and spines take the worst of transit, so art books and photobooks need careful packing.
- Books classify under different tariff headings, and several countries apply a reduced or zero rate to books specifically. Worth checking if you are ordering a lot.
