We pack everything in your warehouse into one shipment and send it to your country.

## How it works

1. Select the items you want to ship from your [warehouse storage](https://koreahaul.com/account/warehouse-storage).
2. Choose handling options per item and packing options for the shipment.
3. Confirm your delivery address and declared value.
4. We pack, weigh, and measure the box, then send you carrier prices.
5. Pick a carrier, pay, and get your tracking number.

## Handling options

Chosen per item or per package.

| Option | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inclusions Basic | ₩1,000 / unit | Album purchases only. Photocards and POBs only. |
| Inclusions Premium | ₩2,000 / unit | Album purchases only. Choose exactly which inclusions to keep. |
| Extra Protection | ₩3,000 / unit | Additional bubble wrap and protective padding. |
| Vacuum Packing | ₩6,000 / unit | Only if size permits. Compresses clothing and bedding to lower your chargeable weight. |

## Packing options

Chosen per shipment.

| Option | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optimized Pack | ₩2,000 + ₩1,000 / package | We repack to cut volume and cost. Includes basic protection and prepacking media. Original parcel boxes may be removed, never sealed packaging. |
| Remove Original Box | ₩3,000 + ₩2,000 / package | We discard the original retail or parcel box during repacking. **Currently not available.** |
| Priority Pack | ₩5,000 / shipment | Your shipment is packed and ready for dispatch within 24 hours. |
| Package Split | ₩5,000 / split package | Create multiple outbound packages from items stored in your warehouse. |
| Adult Signature Required | ₩6,000 | An adult must sign on delivery. FedEx only. |
| Shipping Insurance | 2% of the declared value | Covers loss or damage in transit. FedEx and selected Standard Shipping destinations. |

## Good to know

- Everything goes in one box wherever the carrier's size and weight rules allow.
- Standard packing takes up to 2 business days.
- Carriers charge the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight. Optimized Pack and Vacuum Packing often lower it.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

## Delivery restrictions

- We cannot deliver to PO boxes or military addresses (APO, FPO, DPO).
- Delivery to islands within the EU is generally not possible.
- Remote area surcharges apply where the carrier classifies your address as remote. We include the surcharge in your shipping quote.

We are working to expand the areas we can serve. If you live in a remote area, please contact us before requesting our services.
