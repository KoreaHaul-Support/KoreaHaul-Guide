## Processing fees

Fees vary by method and are added at checkout. They cover the transaction cost charged by the payment provider.

Wise usually carries the lowest fee. Where it is available, it is often the cheapest way to pay.

## Order Limits

The following limits may apply per order and/or per payment:

- PayPal: maximum KRW 500,000 per order
- Credit Card (3D Secure): maximum KRW 500,000 per order
- Alipay: maximum KRW 500,000 per order
- WeChat Pay: maximum KRW 500,000 per order
- Wise: no standard limit
- Western Union: no standard limit
- Bank Transfer: no standard limit

If your order is of higher value and needs a higher payment limit for your payment method, contact us at [support@koreahaul.com](mailto:support@koreahaul.com) for prior approval before you pay.

**KRW 500,000 is roughly USD 370.** The limit is set in Korean won, so the amount in your currency moves with the exchange rate.

Additional verification may be required for high-value transactions.

KoreaHaul reserves the right to impose lower or higher limits, request additional verification, require a specific payment method, or refuse a transaction based on destination, order value, risk assessment, compliance requirements, or payment provider restrictions.
