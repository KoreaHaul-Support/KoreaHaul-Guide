Shop yourself and use our warehouse as your Korean delivery address.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

No service fee. You pay only for storage past your free days and for international shipping.

</div>

## How do I place a Receive For Me request?

1. Use the provided warehouse address as your shipping address when ordering from the seller.
2. Create a [Receive For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/receive-for-me/create) in your dashboard. The domestic tracking number is required.

Your package includes up to 45 days of free storage. Unboxing media is normally ₩2,000 per package, and is free during our launch promotion.

## Good to know

- One request can hold several orders from different stores.
- Tell us the declared value of each item. We use it for insurance and for your customs paperwork.
- If a package arrives without a matching request, we will contact you to submit one.

## Returns

- You arrange the return directly with the seller: contact them, get approval, and book the pickup yourself.
- We repack and hand the parcel over at the warehouse.
- **₩5,000 per return request.** All related costs, including return shipping, are paid by you.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

Without a Postbox code we cannot match your package to your account, which causes delays. We hold it as an unidentified package and list it on our [Notice page](https://koreahaul.com/support/notice) - check there and claim it.

</div>
