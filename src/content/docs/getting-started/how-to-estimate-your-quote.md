All prices in Korean won (KRW).

You pay twice: first for the purchase, then for international shipping. Add up the parts below to estimate what your order will cost, from the store to your door.

## Step 1: Purchase invoice (Buy For Me)

| Part | What to add |
| --- | --- |
| Item price | The price on the Korean store. |
| Korean domestic shipping | What the store charges to deliver to our warehouse. Often free above a set amount. |
| Buy For Me fee | Basic ₩2,000 or Standard ₩5,000 per product line. Premium ₩30,000 per unit. |

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

<p class="kh-callout-title">Launch promotion</p>

Basic and Standard are **₩0** for requests submitted by 31 December 2026. Premium is not included.

</div>

Using Receive For Me? You buy the items yourself, so skip this step.

## Step 2: Shipping invoice (Ship For Me)

### 1. Find your chargeable weight

Carriers charge the higher of:

- **Actual weight** - what the package weighs on the scale.
- **Volumetric weight** - L × W × H (cm) ÷ 5000.

For example, a 40 × 30 × 25 cm box weighing 3 kg is 6 kg volumetric, so you pay for 6 kg.

### 2. Check the shipping price

Enter your country and chargeable weight in the Shipping Calculator on [KoreaHaul](https://koreahaul.com/). Import charges and remote area surcharges will be added separately if applicable. Compare carriers on [Carriers & Rates](/shipping/carriers-and-rates/).

### 3. Add the options you want

| Option | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Inclusions Basic / Inclusions Premium | ₩1,000 / ₩2,000 per unit |
| Extra Protection | ₩3,000 per unit |
| Vacuum Packing | ₩6,000 per unit |
| Optimized Pack | ₩2,000 + ₩1,000 per package. Includes prepacking photos or video. |
| Priority Pack | ₩5,000 per shipment |
| Package Split | ₩5,000 per split package |
| Adult Signature Required | ₩6,000 |
| Shipping Insurance | 2% of declared value |

All options are optional. See [Fees](/getting-started/fees/) for what each one includes.

### 4. Add storage if needed

The first 45 days are free. After that, ₩1,000 per day per package, or ₩2,000 per day if the dimensional weight is 10 kg or more. Storage appears as an Additional Charge on your shipping invoice.

### 5. Add import charges

- **DDP:** you pay duty, tax and a clearance fee at checkout.
    - International Standard Shipping: ₩2,000 to ₩5,000 clearance fee
    - FedEx International Connect Plus: ₩6,000 clearance fee, or ₩12,000 if the estimated import charges are more than ₩120,000
- **DAP:** Any applicable duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees must be paid to the carrier or customs authority upon arrival.

Duty depends on your country's duty-free threshold, the declared value and the HS code. See [Duty & Tax Calculation](/shipping/duty-and-tax-calculation/) and your country in the [Country Guide](/country-guide/).

## Step 3: Payment processing fee

A processing fee is added at checkout on both invoices. It depends on how you pay. Wise usually carries the lowest fee. See [Payment Methods](/getting-started/payment-methods/).

Limits may apply depending on the payment method.

## Example

- **Items:** one album at ₩22,000 and one light stick at ₩45,000
- **Stores:** two different stores, Basic Proxy
- **Arrival:** 2 packages at our warehouse
- **Carrier:** FedEx International Connect Plus
- **Box:** 30 × 25 × 20 cm, 1.2 kg on the scale
- **Volumetric weight:** 30 × 25 × 20 ÷ 5000 = 3 kg
- **Chargeable weight:** 3 kg

| Part | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Items (album ₩22,000 + light stick ₩45,000) | ₩67,000 |
| Korean domestic shipping (2 stores × ₩3,000) | ₩6,000 |
| Buy For Me fee (Basic, launch promotion) | ₩0 |
| **Purchase invoice** | **₩73,000**, plus payment fee |
| Shipping, 3 kg (example rate) | ₩50,000 |
| Inclusions Basic (1 album) | ₩1,000 |
| Optimized Pack (2 packages: ₩2,000 + 2 × ₩1,000) | ₩4,000 |
| Clearance fee | DDP: ₩6,000 / DAP: ₩0 |
| Duty and tax | Depends on your country |
| **Shipping invoice (DDP)** | **₩61,000**, plus duty, tax and payment fee |
| **Shipping invoice (DAP)** | **₩55,000**, plus payment fee. Duty, tax and clearance fees are paid on arrival. |

## Your real quote

When your items reach our warehouse, we weigh and measure them and send you a quote with every available carrier side by side. A shipping quote is valid for **5 days**.

## Tips to pay less

- Ship everything together in one shipment.
- Use Optimized Pack or Vacuum Packing to cut volumetric weight.
- Ship before your 45 free storage days run out.
- Pay with Wise where it is available.

More tips in [How to spend less on shipping](/shopping-tips/how-to-spend-less-on-shipping/).
