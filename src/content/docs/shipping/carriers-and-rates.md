## Restrictions

**Branded products.** International Standard Shipping does not accept them. Nike, Adidas, LEGO, Pokémon, Casio, Chanel, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and similar must go by FedEx, EMS Korea Post, or SF Express.

**Batteries.** Must be built into the device. Loose batteries and power banks cannot be shipped by any service. Every carrier that accepts them caps it at 1 to 2 devices per shipment.

- **FedEx** - accepted
- **SF Express** - accepted
- **International Standard Shipping** - accepted, except to Japan, China, and Hong Kong
- **EMS Korea Post** - not accepted, including built-in

## How shipping weight is calculated

Carriers charge the higher of two numbers:

- **Actual weight** - what the packed box weighs.
- **Volumetric weight** - how much space it takes up. L × W × H (cm) ÷ 5000.

We show both and mark which applies. Optimized Pack and Vacuum Packing often bring the volumetric weight down.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

A 40 × 30 × 25 cm box of clothing weighs 3 kg on the scale, but 40 × 30 × 25 ÷ 5000 = 6 kg volumetric. You are charged for 6 kg.

</div>

## Coverage

- FedEx includes complimentary cover up to USD $300 for loss and damage.
- International Standard Shipping, EMS Korea Post, and SF Express do not cover damage in transit. Loss cover on International Standard Shipping varies by destination, from $200 to none.
- Insurance costs 2% of declared value and is available on FedEx only.

See Insurance and claims for the full picture, including the destinations with no cover.

## Tracking

- You get a tracking number as soon as the box leaves our warehouse.
- The carrier's site can take 1-2 business days to show the first scan.
- No movement for more than five business days? Send us your request number and we will open a trace.

## Rates

- We book carriers, we do not operate them. Carrier delays, routing decisions, and service suspensions are outside our control.
- Rate changes are announced at least 5 days before they take effect. Pricing errors are the exception - we correct those as soon as we find them.

## Delivery restrictions

- We cannot deliver to PO boxes or military addresses (APO, FPO, DPO).
- Delivery to islands within the EU is generally not possible.
- Remote area surcharges apply where the carrier classifies your address as remote. We include the surcharge in your shipping quote.

We are working to expand the areas we can serve. If you live in a remote area, please contact us before requesting our services.
