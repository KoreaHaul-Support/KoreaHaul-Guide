International Standard Shipping does not accept branded products. This applies to every International Standard Shipping route, including routes carried by YunExpress. Restricted products include:

- Every brand on the list below.
- Any brand registered with international customs for intellectual property protection, even if it is not on the list.

Ship branded products by FedEx, EMS Korea Post, or SF Express instead.

## Luxury goods

International Standard Shipping can carry luxury goods only with a genuine authorization letter from the brand. The carrier and customs must verify the letter before the parcel ships. Without a verified letter, the parcel is refused. In most cases, FedEx or EMS Korea Post is the easier option.

## What happens if a restricted brand is found

The carrier handles it under its counterfeit and prohibited items rules. The parcel may be refused, returned or held.
