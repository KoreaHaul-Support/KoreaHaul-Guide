Posted 5 September 2026

The launch promotion continues. Every Receive For Me request gets photos and an unboxing video at no charge, instead of the usual ₩2,000 per parcel.

Buy For Me already includes unboxing media on every service tier, at no extra cost.

## What is included

- Photos or video of the package as it arrived and of the contents once opened.
- A warehouse note where we may spot damage, a missing item, or the wrong option.

## Why it matters

The unboxing video is what supports a claim. If something is wrong with an order, you have **5 days from receiving the unboxing media** to report it.

## When this ends

We will post here at least 5 days before the promotion ends. Nothing changes for requests already submitted.
