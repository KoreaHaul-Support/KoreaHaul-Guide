Posted 13 October 2026

From 12 October 2026, refunds to your original payment method have a handling fee:

- 2% of the refund amount
- plus any processing fees charged by the payment provider (for example PayPal or your card issuer)

Refunds issued as Store Credit incur no processing fees. You can use Store Credit to pay for all our services, including product payments and international shipping.
