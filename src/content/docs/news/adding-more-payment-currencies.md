Posted 5 October 2026

We're adding more currencies to our payment methods so you can pay in your own currency.

While we make this change, checkout may be briefly interrupted. Some payment methods or currencies may not be available for a short time.

## What to do

- If a payment method doesn't load, try again later or pick another method.
- Payments you've already made are not affected.
- If you see an error, email [support@koreahaul.com](mailto:support@koreahaul.com) with your request number (for example BF260900059).

We'll update this notice when the new currencies are live. Thank you for your patience.
