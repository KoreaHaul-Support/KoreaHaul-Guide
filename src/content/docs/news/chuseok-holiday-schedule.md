Our warehouse is closed from **24 to 27 September**. Shipments already packed go out on 28 September.

</div>

Chuseok is one of the two largest holidays in Korea. Stores, couriers, and our warehouse all pause, and the slowdown runs for about a week either side of the closure itself.

## What this means for you

- **Buy For Me orders** placed after 24 September will most likely be purchased after the holiday.
- **Korean domestic deliveries** slow sharply in the week before. Sellers often stop dispatching two or three days out.
- **Packages arriving during the closure** are checked in on 28 September.
- **International shipments** already paid for are unaffected once handed to the carrier.

## If you need something before the holiday

Order at least two weeks ahead, and use Priority Pack to have your shipment packed within 24 hours of your request.

## Customer support

Email is monitored during the closure but replies will be slower than the usual one business day.
