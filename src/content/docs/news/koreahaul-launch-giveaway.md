## Terms and conditions

- Purchased items must be shipped with FedEx between Oct 1 and Nov 30, 2026 to qualify. Shipping and import charges are paid by the customer, and gifts are declared at retail value for customs.
- Limit one promotional gift per customer during the promotion period, from the highest tier you reach (tiers are not cumulative).
- Only Buy For Me product prices count toward your spend (shipping, fees, taxes and duties excluded).
- Eligibility is based on final paid product value after cancellations and refunds.
- Orders from KREAM, Soldout, Bunjang, Pocamarket, Daangn (Karrot), Joongonara, X, Instagram or similar platforms are not eligible.
- Items with member versions (photocards, posters) are sent at random - members cannot be chosen.
- Olive Young coupons must be used by Dec 31, 2026.
- First come, first served: gifts go out in the order shipments are sent, while supplies last.
