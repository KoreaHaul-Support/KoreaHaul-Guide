*Posted 18 September 2026*

Since July 8, 2026, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) requires electronic filing (eFiling) of safety certificate information for certain consumer products imported into the United States, including some personal-use shipments. This is not a new safety requirement. It changes how existing certification information is submitted to U.S. authorities.

## Products that may be affected

- Toys and plush dolls
- Children's and baby products (chairs, furniture, gates, strollers, infant sleep products, pacifiers)
- Certain clothing, hats, shoes, and textile products
- Certain electronics and charging-related products
- Products containing batteries, including button cell and coin batteries
- Mattresses and furniture
- Carpets and rugs
- Bicycles, bicycle helmets, and sporting goods
- Imitation jewelry
- Lighters and matchbooks
- Other products subject to CPSC regulations

This list is based on CPSC's January 2026 guidance (see Useful links) and is not complete. Whether eFiling is required depends on the product type, manufacturer, intended use, and applicable U.S. regulations. A 14+ age label does not automatically make a product exempt.

## K-pop merchandise

Most albums, photocards, photobooks, and stationery products are not expected to be affected.

Additional information or certification details may be requested for:

- Character dolls and plush toys
- Toy-related merchandise
- Light sticks and other battery-powered products
- Electronic merchandise
- Products marketed or intended for children

## What to do

1. Ask the seller or manufacturer whether the product is subject to CPSC requirements.
2. If applicable, obtain the certificate (CPC or GCC) details, including product identifiers, manufacture information, testing information, and contact details. These must come from the manufacturer or seller.
3. If the product is registered in the CPSC Product Registry, complete the FedEx Registered Product Form and send it to us before shipment.
4. If the product is not registered, complete the FedEx Not Registered Product Form and send it to us before shipment.

Products bought on second-hand or peer-to-peer platforms - Bunjang, Karrot, Joongonara, X, Instagram sellers and similar - may not have certification or compliance information available. Check before you buy. If the required information cannot be provided, customs clearance may be delayed, suspended, or refused, and the shipment may be returned.
