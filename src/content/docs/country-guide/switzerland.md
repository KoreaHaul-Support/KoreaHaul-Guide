## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **VAT:** 8.1% (one of the lowest in Europe). A small-value relief applies: if the VAT owed would be under CHF 5, it is generally not collected.
- **Duty:**
    - Switzerland abolished customs duties on industrial products in HS Chapters 25-97. Most merchandise (apparel, footwear, bags, cosmetics, albums, toys, electronics, stationery) is generally duty-free regardless of origin.
    - Customs duty can still apply to agricultural products, including many foods; Swiss agricultural duty is generally weight-based.
- **Customs value / basis:** VAT is calculated on the goods plus transport and any applicable duty.

## What it costs

Example shipment:

- Goods: CHF 100
- International shipping: CHF 30
- Swiss import VAT: 8.1% of the applicable import-tax base = about CHF 10.53
- VAT relief: generally not collected if the amount due is under CHF 5
- Customs duty: generally CHF 0 for industrial products; agricultural products can carry weight-based duty
- Carrier or customs-clearance fee: may apply

Estimated VAT: about CHF 10.53, plus any carrier fee and any applicable agricultural-product duty.

*Illustrative only. Final VAT, customs charges, and clearance fees are set by Swiss authorities and the carrier or broker.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?**
    - **Prepaid (DDP):** in most cases no. Additional charges may apply due to incorrect information, inaccurate documents, undervaluation, or other import-related issues.
    - **Recipient-paid (DAP):** you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Most industrial products are duty-free. Swiss import VAT is generally not collected if the VAT amount due is under CHF 5.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping (it can raise the declared value).
