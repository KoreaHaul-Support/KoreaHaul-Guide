## Delivery

- **FedEx International Priority** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 2-3 business days.
- **SF Express** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 3-7 business days.
- **International Standard Shipping** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No built-in batteries to Japan. Loss cover up to $40. Estimated 5-10 business days.
- **EMS** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No batteries of any kind, including built-in. Estimated 3-7 business days.

## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **Personal-use 60% rule:** for personal imports, the customs value is generally assessed at about 60% of the overseas retail price, not the full price. Japan has decided to abolish this rule with effect from 1 April 2028. It still applies until then.
- **JPY 10,000 relief:**
    - Personal imports with a total customs value of JPY 10,000 or less are generally exempt from customs duty and consumption tax.
    - For own personal-use imports, Customs generally assesses value at 60% of the overseas retail price.
    - As a rough guide, a single purchase priced up to about JPY 16,666 may fall within the threshold, but Customs considers the total customs value of the import.
    - The relief does not generally apply to designated goods, including leather bags, handbags and gloves; knitted apparel; ski boots, leather shoes and leather-soled footwear; and certain other specified goods.
- **Consumption tax:** 10% standard; 8% on food and beverages.
- **Duty:** varies by product and tariff classification; many consumer goods are low or zero.
- **Customs value** for personal imports is generally the 60% assessed value; commercial imports use the transaction value.

## What it costs

**Small personal order (within the relief):**

- Overseas price: JPY 15,000
- Customs value (60% rule): JPY 9,000
- Duty and consumption tax: generally none, provided the goods qualify for the JPY 10,000 exemption and are not in an excluded category

**Larger order (above the relief):**

- Overseas price: JPY 30,000
- Customs value (60% rule): JPY 18,000
- Duty: varies by product and tariff classification.
- Import consumption tax: generally 10% of the taxable import value (including any duty); Customs applies statutory calculation and rounding. Food and qualifying non-alcoholic beverages may use the 8% reduced rate.
- Illustrative consumption tax: about JPY 1,800 if the taxable import value is JPY 18,000 and no duty applies.

*Illustrative only. Final duty and tax are set by Japan Customs and the carrier.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted; Japan limits personal quantities)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Japan are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Often, but not always.
    - Personal imports with a customs value of JPY 10,000 or less are generally exempt from duty and consumption tax.
    - Customs assesses personal-use imports at 60% of the overseas retail price.
    - Leather goods, knitted apparel, and footwear are excluded from the relief.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes. Consolidating can save on shipping, but a higher total customs value can push the shipment above the JPY 10,000 relief threshold.
