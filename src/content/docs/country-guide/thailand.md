## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **VAT (7%):**
    - Thailand charges 7% VAT on imports, including low-value shipments.
    - The previous VAT relief for low-value imports has ended.
- **Import duty:**
    - From 1 January 2026, Thailand generally collects import duty on online purchases valued above THB 1. The former THB 1,500 exemption has ended.
    - Thai Customs can assess duty and VAT at import, plus any carrier or clearance fees.
- **Customs value** is generally based on the CIF value (goods, international freight, insurance).

## What it costs

**Low-value order** (an online purchase valued above THB 1):

- Import duty: depends on tariff classification and customs value
- VAT: 7%, generally on the customs value plus applicable duty
- Carrier or clearance fee: may apply

**Example:**

- Customs value, CIF: THB 1,000
- Illustrative duty rate: 10%
- Import duty: THB 100
- VAT at 7% on THB 1,100: THB 77
- Estimated import charges: THB 177, plus any carrier or clearance fee

*Illustrative only. The final duty rate and tax assessment are determined by Thai Customs. A different tariff classification can change the duty rate.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes (vapes are banned in Thailand)
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted; may need Thai FDA approval)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Thailand are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** No. Thailand charges 7% VAT on all imports, and since 1 January 2026 import duty can apply to online purchases valued above THB 1.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes. Consolidating can save shipping costs, but it increases the customs value used to calculate duty and VAT.
