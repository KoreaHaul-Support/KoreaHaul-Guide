## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **US$400 franchise:**
    - The first **5 shipments per person per calendar year**, each up to **US$400 FOB**, are exempt from import duty and the statistical fee. Only IVA and internal taxes apply, where relevant.
    - This applies to **both** routes: courier (FedEx) and postal Puerta a Puerta (EMS). It comes from Decreto 1065/2024, in force since 2 December 2024.
    - Above US$400 FOB, the general import charges apply to the excess.
    - Past the 5-shipment yearly limit, the general charges apply to the full value even if the shipment is under US$400.
- **Courier limits:** non-commercial use, up to 3 units of the same item, up to 50 kg per package, and up to US$3,000 FOB per shipment.
- **Puerta a Puerta limits:** non-commercial use, up to 3 units of the same item, up to 20 kg per package, within the same US$3,000 ceiling.
- **Sales tax:** 21% IVA.
- **Customs value** is generally based on CIF value and can include goods, international freight, insurance, and other applicable additions.

## What it costs

Example qualifying shipment, on either FedEx or EMS:

- Goods value: US$100 FOB
- International shipping: US$30
- Import duty and statistical fee: generally exempt, because the goods value is within the US$400 franchise and within the 5-per-year limit
- IVA: 21% may apply; the taxable base is set under Argentine customs and tax rules
- Carrier fee: may apply

Above US$400 FOB, the general import charges apply to the excess. After the fifth shipment of the year, they apply to the full value.

*Illustrative only. The exemption is based on FOB goods value, not the delivered total.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Argentina are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order duty-free?** Partly.
    - The first 5 shipments per person per year, each up to US$400 FOB, are exempt from import duty and the statistical fee.
    - This applies on both the courier and postal routes.
    - IVA and internal taxes still apply.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping, but a larger value can push you past the US$400 allowance.
