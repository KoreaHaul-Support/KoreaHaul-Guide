## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **De minimis (US$3 FOB):**
    - Shipments with an FOB value of US$3 or less are exempt from import duty, but Indonesian VAT still applies.
    - FOB means the value of the goods, excluding international freight and insurance.
- **Above US$3 up to US$1,500 FOB:**
    - Most goods: import duty 7.5%.
    - These come from PMK 96/2023 as amended by PMK 4/2025. The rate for a specific item depends on its HS classification, so treat the figures above as a guide.
    - VAT applies. For most imported shipments, the effective VAT burden is generally 11% under the current import-tax calculation.
    - VAT is calculated using the applicable import-tax base, which generally includes customs value and import duty.
    - Income tax (PPh) is generally exempt in this value range.
- **Above US$1,500 FOB:**
    - A formal import declaration is generally required, with duty by tariff classification and rate, plus VAT and any other applicable taxes or charges.
- **Customs value** is generally based on the CIF value (goods, international freight, insurance).

## What it costs

Example: ordinary consumer-goods shipment

- Goods value, FOB: USD 100
- International freight and insurance: USD 20
- Customs value, CIF: USD 120
- Import duty at 7.5%: about USD 9.00
- VAT: calculated on the applicable import-tax base, generally including customs value and import duty
- Illustrative VAT: about USD 14.19, using an effective 11% rate on USD 129
- Income tax (PPh): generally exempt for shipments above USD 3 and up to USD 1,500 FOB
- Carrier or clearance fee: may apply

Estimated duty and VAT: about USD 23.19, plus any carrier fee.

*Illustrative only. Customs uses its own exchange rate, valuation rules, and applicable tax calculations. Special categories (bags, textiles/garments, footwear, cosmetics, and watches) can have different duty rates.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted; may need BPOM approval)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Indonesia are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Not fully.
    - Shipments with an FOB value of US$3 or less are exempt from import duty, but VAT still applies.
    - Above US$3 and up to US$1,500, duty is usually 7.5% and VAT applies.
    - Income tax (PPh) is generally exempt in that range. Some products have special duty rates.
- **Why is the tax quote different from the item price?** Indonesia uses the FOB value to determine the shipment threshold, but Customs generally uses CIF value (goods, international freight, and insurance) to calculate import charges.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping (it can raise the declared value).
