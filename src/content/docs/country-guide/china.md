## Delivery

- **SF Express** - fast, wide coverage across China. Import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 3-7 business days.
- **FedEx International Priority** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 2-3 business days.
- **International Standard Shipping** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No built-in batteries to China. Loss cover up to $40. Estimated 5-8 business days.
- **EMS** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No batteries of any kind, including built-in. Estimated 3-7 business days.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

**Recipient-paid (DAP/DDU)** = You pay import charges when your package arrives in your country.

</div>

Import charges may include duties, taxes, customs handling fees, and other import-related charges.

To estimate the shipping cost and transit time, use the Shipping Calculator at [koreahaul.com](https://koreahaul.com/).

*Maximum weight per shipment: 10 kg on SF Express, 30 kg on FedEx International Priority, 5 kg on International Standard Shipping, 20 kg on EMS.*

## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **Personal postal articles tax (parcel tax):**
    - Parcels for reasonable personal use are taxed under China's simplified personal-article import-tax system.
    - The integrated tax rate is normally 13%, 20%, or 50%, depending on the Customs category and taxable-value description.
    - 13%: books and publications, food and non-alcoholic beverages, and certain IT products.
    - 20%: textiles and textile articles, sporting goods, and many other goods not in the 13% or 50% categories.
    - 50%: tobacco, alcohol, precious jewellery, golf products, high-end watches, high-end cosmetics, and batteries.
    - Classification, taxable value, and the final rate are determined by China Customs.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**K-beauty can land at 50%.** Cosmetics and skincare are taxed at 20%, but count as high-end at 50% once the taxable value reaches CNY 10 per ml or gram, or CNY 15 per sheet for masks. Many Korean serums, creams, and premium sheet masks cross that line.

</div>

- **CNY 50 exemption:** if the parcel tax owed is CNY 50 or less, it is generally exempted.
- **Personal-shipment value limit (CNY 2,000):**
    - Personal articles imported by post or courier for reasonable personal use are generally limited to CNY 2,000 per shipment.
    - Above CNY 2,000, a shipment may need formal goods-import clearance or may be returned.
    - A single indivisible personal-use item above the limit may still be considered for personal-article clearance if China Customs accepts it.
- **Customs value** is generally based on the value of the goods, subject to Customs assessment.

*Illustrative only. China Customs determines the item's taxable value, classification, applicable rate, and whether the shipment qualifies as reasonable personal use.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Publications, media, or maps that may be restricted; cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to China are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay parcel tax and any fees at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** If the parcel tax owed is CNY 50 or less, it is generally exempt; otherwise the parcel-tax rate for the product category applies.
- **Do I need to provide ID?** Usually. Carriers and Customs commonly require the recipient's Chinese resident ID or passport details for real-name clearance. Missing or mismatched information can delay, hold, or return a shipment.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes, but consolidation increases the declared value. Personal shipments entering China are generally limited to CNY 2,000 per shipment and must be for reasonable personal use. A shipment above the limit may need formal goods-import clearance or may be returned.
