## Delivery

- **SF Express** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 3-6 business days.
- **FedEx International Priority** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 2-4 business days.
- **International Standard Shipping** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Loss cover up to $50. Estimated 5-10 business days.
- **EMS** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No batteries of any kind, including built-in. Estimated 4-8 business days.

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--note">

**Recipient-paid (DAP/DDU)** = You pay import charges when your package arrives in your country.

</div>

Import charges may include duties, taxes, customs handling fees, and other import-related charges.

To estimate the shipping cost and transit time, use the Shipping Calculator at [koreahaul.com](https://koreahaul.com/).

*Maximum weight per shipment: 10 kg on SF Express, 30 kg on FedEx International Priority, 5 kg on International Standard Shipping, 20 kg on EMS.*

## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **De minimis (NT$2,000):**
    - Goods with a customs value of NT$2,000 (about US$62) or less are generally free of import duty and business tax.
    - This exemption is generally available for up to 6 qualifying duty-free imports per importer in each half-year. If the same importer receives it more than 6 times in a half-year, Customs may treat them as a frequent importer and the exemption no longer applies. The count resets on 1 January and 1 July.
    - The low-value exemption does not apply to tobacco, alcohol, agricultural products subject to tariff quotas, or goods imported frequently.
- **Business tax (VAT):** 5%.
- **Import duty:** depends on the tariff classification and customs value; above the threshold, duty and 5% business tax apply.
- **Customs value** is generally based on the CIF value (goods, international freight, insurance).

## What it costs

**Small order (up to NT$2,000, within the frequency limit):**

- Import duty and business tax: generally none

**Larger order (above NT$2,000, or over the frequency limit):**

- Import duty: by product and tariff classification
- Business tax at 5%: generally calculated on the customs value plus import duty and any applicable commodity or tobacco-and-alcohol taxes.
- Plus any carrier or clearance fee

*Illustrative only. Final duty and tax are set by Taiwan Customs and the carrier.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted; personal quantities may be limited)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Taiwan are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Often.
    - Goods with a customs value of NT$2,000 or less are generally exempt from import duty, business tax, and commodity tax.
    - Limited to 6 qualifying imports per importer in each half-year.
    - Tobacco, alcohol, tariff-quota agricultural goods, and frequent imports do not qualify.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping, but a larger value can push you past the NT$2,000 threshold.
