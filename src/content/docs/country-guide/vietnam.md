## Delivery

- **SF Express** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 4-7 business days.
- **FedEx International Priority** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). Estimated 2-4 business days.
- **EMS** - import charges paid by the recipient (DAP). No batteries of any kind, including built-in. Estimated 5-8 business days.

## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **Low-value express shipments:**
    - Since 18 February 2025 (Decision 01/2025/QD-TTg), Vietnam no longer grants the former VAT exemption for low-value goods imported by express delivery. VAT can apply regardless of shipment value.
    - A separate customs-duty exemption may still apply to qualifying low-value express shipments, depending on the current law, shipment value, product, clearance method, and Customs assessment.
- **VAT:** 10% standard; some goods may receive different treatment.
- **Customs value** is generally based on the CIF value (goods, international freight, insurance).

## What it costs

On a US$100 order with US$30 shipping (example CIF value US$130):

- Import duty: may be exempt for a qualifying low-value express shipment. If payable, the rate depends on tariff classification and customs value.
- VAT at 10%: about US$13 if the CIF value is US$130 and no duty applies. If duty applies, VAT is on the CIF value plus duty.
- **Estimated: about US$13 where no duty is payable**, plus any carrier fee; otherwise add applicable duty and the resulting additional VAT.

*Illustrative only. Final duty and tax are set by Vietnam Customs and the carrier.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Publications, media, or maps that may be restricted; cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to Vietnam are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Not entirely.
    - Since 18 February 2025, VAT applies to low-value express imports.
    - A customs-duty exemption may still apply to qualifying low-value shipments.
    - Otherwise duty depends on the product, customs value, and origin.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping (it can raise the declared value).
