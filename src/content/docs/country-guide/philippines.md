## Customs, duty and tax

<div class="kh-callout kh-callout--caution">

**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.

</div>

- **De minimis (PHP 10,000 FOB/FCA):**
    - Goods with an FOB or FCA value of PHP 10,000 (about US$180) or less are generally exempt from Philippine customs duty and import taxes.
    - FOB/FCA is generally the value of the goods before international freight and insurance.
    - This is a threshold test, not an allowance deducted from a larger shipment. If the FOB/FCA value exceeds PHP 10,000, duty and VAT can be assessed on the importation.
    - Alcohol and tobacco can still be subject to excise tax even within the PHP 10,000 value. Restricted or regulated goods can need permits or separate requirements.
- **Above PHP 10,000:** import duty and 12% VAT may apply. Duty depends on the tariff classification, customs value, origin, and applicable rules.
- **Import VAT:** 12% of the total landed cost; the VAT base generally includes the customs/dutiable value plus duty, excise where applicable, and relevant charges before release.
- **Customs value:** when duty and VAT apply, Customs generally assesses the shipment on its dutiable or landed value (goods, international freight, insurance, and applicable additions).

## What it costs

Illustrative: US$100 goods value plus US$30 international shipping.

- **De minimis check:** the PHP 10,000 threshold applies to the FOB/FCA goods value, excluding international freight and insurance. The peso result depends on the Customs exchange rate.
- **If the goods value is at or below PHP 10,000 (and the shipment qualifies):**
    - Duty and VAT: generally none
    - You may still pay any carrier, handling, or delivery fee
- **If the goods value exceeds PHP 10,000:**
    - Customs/dutiable value generally includes goods, freight, insurance, and applicable additions
    - Duty: depends on the tariff classification
    - VAT at 12%: on the total landed cost, including applicable duty

*Illustrative only. Final assessment is determined by Philippine Customs and the carrier.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted; may need FDA approval)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?** Yes. All services to the Philippines are recipient-paid (DAP), so you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Often - goods with an FOB/FCA value of PHP 10,000 or less are generally exempt from duty and import tax. Alcohol and tobacco can still carry excise.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine orders?** Yes - consolidating saves on shipping, but a larger value can push you past the PHP 10,000 threshold.
