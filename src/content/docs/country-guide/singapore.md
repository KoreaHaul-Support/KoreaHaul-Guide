## Customs, duty and tax

:::caution
**Customs clearance is your responsibility.** Before requesting our services, confirm that your items can be imported into your country. We ship what you request, but we cannot guarantee it will clear customs.
:::

- **GST:** 9%.
- **Import GST relief (air and post):**
    - Singapore generally grants import GST relief for eligible air or postal shipments with a total CIF value of S$400 or less. The relief does not apply to liquor or tobacco, and carrier or clearance fees may still apply.
    - It also does not apply to goods imported by sea or land.
    - If the total CIF value of an air/post shipment exceeds S$400, GST is generally collected at import on the full taxable value.
- **Customs duty:**
    - Most ordinary consumer goods are non-dutiable (GST only).
    - Duty applies mainly to four categories: intoxicating liquor, tobacco products, motor vehicles, and petroleum products/biodiesel blends.
- **GST at import:** generally 9% of the total CIF value plus any applicable customs or excise duty.

## What it costs

**Small air or postal shipment (CIF S$400 or less):**

- Import GST relief generally applies, so no GST at the border for eligible goods.
- Carrier or clearance fees may still apply, even where import GST relief applies.

**Air or postal shipment above S$400 CIF (example CIF S$500):**

- GST at 9% of CIF value plus any applicable duty: about S$45.
- Carrier, permit, or clearance charges where applicable.

*Illustrative only. The final amount depends on the product, transport method, CIF value, customs classification, and shipment information.*

## What we can't ship

- Flammables and pressurized items (perfume, cologne, nail polish, aerosols, lighters, hand warmers)
- Loose batteries and power banks
- Alcohol, tobacco, and vapes
- Fresh or perishable food, meat, plants, and seeds
- Weapons, replicas, and self-defense sprays; counterfeit goods
- Medicines and supplements (often restricted)
- Sunscreen and other regulated cosmetic or drug products that may have extra import requirements
- Chewing gum (restricted in Singapore), cash, gift cards, and precious metals

See the [Prohibited and Restricted Items](https://koreahaul.com/shipping-info/restricted) page for full details.

## Questions

- **Do I pay on delivery?**
    - **Prepaid (DDP):** in most cases no. Additional charges may apply due to incorrect information, inaccurate documents, undervaluation, or other import-related issues.
    - **Recipient-paid (DAP):** you pay import charges at clearance or delivery.
- **Is a small order tax-free?** Often.
    - Air or postal shipments with a total CIF value of S$400 or less generally get import GST relief.
    - Above S$400 CIF, 9% GST applies at import.
    - Most goods have no customs duty. Liquor and tobacco are excluded from the relief.
- **Can you buy from any Korean site?** Most of them. Submit the link through a [Buy For Me request](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create) and we will check.
- **Can I combine several orders?** Yes. Consolidation can reduce shipping costs, but it can increase the total CIF value. For air or postal shipments above S$400 CIF, 9% GST is generally collected at import on the full taxable value.
