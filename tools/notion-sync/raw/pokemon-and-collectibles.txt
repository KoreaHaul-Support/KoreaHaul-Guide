## Packing
- Sealed booster boxes need careful packing - crushed corners destroy the value.
- Add Extra Protection and we pack them in a separate plastic container.
- Singles should ship in a toploader or sleeve.
- Blind boxes cannot be opened to check the variant. Order the full set if you need a specific figure.
## Customs
Trading cards and collectible figures classify differently by country, which changes the duty rate. See HS codes for common items.
