## How pricing works now
**Korean won is the base currency.** We charge what the Korean seller charged, with nothing added.
**Choose your display currency.** Prices are converted at the mid-market rate provided by Wise, so you see what your order actually costs in your own currency.
**No exchange rate markup from us** on product prices, shipping, or service fees. Many forwarding services add 5% or more on conversion. We add nothing.
**Payment processing fees are added at checkout and paid by you.** This covers the transaction cost charged by payment providers.
## Service fees in won
Approximate USD figures are shown for reference and move with the exchange rate.
**Buy For Me** - Basic ₩2,000 (about \$1.50), Standard ₩5,000 (about \$3.70), Premium ₩30,000 (about \$22) per unit. Premium now applies to items over ₩500,000 (about \$370).
**Packing and handling** - Inclusions ₩1,000 and ₩2,000 per unit, Extra Protection ₩3,000, Vacuum Packing ₩6,000, Optimized Pack ₩2,000 + ₩1,000, Priority Pack ₩5,000, Package Split ₩5,000, Adult Signature Required ₩6,000.

**Returns** - ₩5,000 per request, with no handling fee where the seller is at fault. Return shipping is paid by you. A ₩1,000 handling fee applies if the seller cancels before shipment.
**Unboxing media** - ₩2,000 per package, free for Receive For Me during the launch promotion.
**Customs handling (DDP)** - International Standard Shipping ₩2,000 to ₩5,000. FedEx International Connect Plus ₩6,000, or ₩12,000 where estimated import charges exceed ₩120,000. DDP overpayments above ₩5,000 are refunded as Store Credit.
**Shipping insurance** - 2% of declared value, now available on FedEx and selected Standard Shipping destinations.
