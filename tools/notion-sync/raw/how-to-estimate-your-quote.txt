## Step 2: Shipping invoice (Ship For Me)
### 1. Find your chargeable weight
Carriers charge the higher of:
- **Actual weight** - what the package weighs on the scale.
- **Volumetric weight** - L × W × H (cm) ÷ 5000.
For example, a 40 × 30 × 25 cm box weighing 3 kg is 6 kg volumetric, so you pay for 6 kg.
### 2. Check the shipping price
Enter your country and chargeable weight in the Shipping Calculator on [KoreaHaul](https://koreahaul.com/). Remote area surcharges are added later if applicable. Compare carriers on <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dbdc07682c28174a8bdce84cefbb82c"/>.

### 4. Add storage if needed
The first 45 days are free. After that, ₩1,000 per day per package, or ₩2,000 per day if the dimensional weight is 10 kg or more. Storage appears as an Additional Charge on your shipping invoice.
### 5. Add import charges
- **DDP:** you pay duty, tax and a clearance fee at checkout. The clearance fee is ₩2,000 to ₩5,000 on International Standard Shipping. On FedEx International Connect Plus it is ₩6,000, or ₩12,000 if the estimated import charges are more than ₩120,000.
- **DAP:** you pay duty, tax and clearance fees to the carrier when the parcel arrives.
Duty depends on your country's duty-free threshold, the declared value and the HS code. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dbdc07682c2815ba574da5dfbec28e6"/> and your country in the <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dbdc07682c281cf96efc267212e99f2"/>.
## Step 3: Payment processing fee
A processing fee is added at checkout on both invoices. It depends on how you pay. Wise usually carries the lowest fee. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dedc07682c281628995d37e379cbb24"/>.
Limits may apply depending on the payment method.

## Your real quote
When your items reach our warehouse, we weigh and measure them and send you a quote with every available carrier side by side. A shipping quote is valid for **5 days**.
## Tips to pay less
- Ship everything together in one shipment.
- Use Optimized Pack or Vacuum Packing to cut volumetric weight.
- Ship before your 45 free storage days run out.
- Pay with Wise where it is available.
