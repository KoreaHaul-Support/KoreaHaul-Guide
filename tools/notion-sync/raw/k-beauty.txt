The most shipped category out of our warehouse, and the one with the most customs surprises.
## Where to buy
Start with the K-Beauty category on [**KoreaHaul Picks (KH Picks)**](https://koreahaul.com/koreahaul-shop). We have catalogued product names and links there so you can submit a Buy For Me request in a few clicks. These are curated links, not stock - we do not hold products ourselves.
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Buy from official stores only.**
	- Comparison sites and open marketplaces list resellers too.
	- Authenticity cannot be guaranteed from reseller listings.
	- Every K-beauty store in our [Where to Shop](https://koreahaul.com/where-to-shop) directory is official or authorised.
</callout>
- [**Olive Young**](https://oliveyoung.co.kr) - the default. Widest range, frequent sales, own-brand lines that are hard to find elsewhere.
- [**Musinsa Beauty**](https://www.musinsa.com/main/beauty/recommend?gf=A) - growing K-beauty range alongside the fashion side, often with its own discounts.
- **Brand sites** - see [Where to Shop](https://koreahaul.com/where-to-shop) for the full directory of official brand stores.
- **Naver** - brand stores sit at [**brand.naver.com/\[brandname\]**](http://brand.naver.com/[brandname]), for example [medicube](https://brand.naver.com/medicube), [mediheal](https://brand.naver.com/mediheal), or [roundlab](https://brand.naver.com/roundlab). Anything at [smartstore.naver.com](http://smartstore.naver.com) is an independent seller.
- [**Coupang**](https://coupang.com) - good deals, but retail boxes often arrive dented or scuffed. Fine if you are using the product, not if the packaging matters.
## Watch the sale calendar
Olive Young runs major sales roughly quarterly, and brands stack their own events on top. If your order is not urgent, waiting for one beats any price comparison.
## Customs and shipping limits
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**We cannot ship K-beauty to Spain, Mexico, or Turkey.** Customs clearance for cosmetics is close to impossible on those routes, so we do not accept the requests at all.
</callout>
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Limit of 3 per product, per Buy For Me request.** For example, three of the same serum. Want more? Submit another request.
	This limit applies to K-beauty specifically.
	- Korean beauty stores watch for resellers and ban accounts that look like one.
	- Buying in quantity from a single store puts our access at risk.
	- Daiso is the exception: 5 per product, per request.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="yellow_bg">
	**Bulk orders can be treated as commercial imports.**
	- Several countries cap how much cosmetics can enter as personal use.
	- Above that cap, your box needs formal import registration.
	- Clearing it can cost a few thousand dollars on its own, before any fees or testing.
	- Check your country page before ordering in bulk.
</callout>
<callout icon="🇺🇸" color="yellow_bg">
	**Shipping to the United States on International Standard Shipping?** K-beauty must be shipped separately, with a ₩6,000 handling fee. Submit a separate Ship For Me request for them.
</callout>
- **Perfume, nail polish, and aerosols cannot be shipped at all.** No carrier will take them.
- **Sunscreen and SPF cushion compacts** are regulated as over-the-counter drugs in some countries. Where that applies, they cannot be shipped at all. Checking your country's rules is your responsibility.
