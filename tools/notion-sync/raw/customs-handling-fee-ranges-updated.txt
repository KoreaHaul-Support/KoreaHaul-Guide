- Fees can go higher on some destinations. Treat the figures above as the usual range, not a cap.
- EMS Korea Post, SF Express, and FedEx International Priority are DAP only, so no DDP handling fee applies.
- On those services the carrier and your local customs charge you instead, if applicable.
<callout icon="ℹ️" color="gray_bg">

</callout>
## Worth knowing
Where a shipment is refused, returned, or seized, prepaid duties and customs handling fees are not refundable.
This fee applies to DDP only. On DAP the carrier bills you directly on arrival and usually adds its own handling charge.
