Korean prices move constantly. The same item can differ by 30% between stores on the same day.
## Where to compare
- [**Naver Shopping**](https://shopping.naver.com/ns/home) - most Korean sellers. Search the Korean product name and sort by price.
- [**Danawa**](https://danawa.com) - electronics and appliances, with price history charts.
- [**Enuri**](https://enuri.com) - general goods, often catches card-linked discounts.
Naver Shopping is not reachable from every country. If it will not load for you, use Enuri instead.

## What the listed price may hide
The price we pay excludes discounts tied to selected Korean credit cards, points earned from the purchase, and limited coupons issued to select members. These are not included in our Buy For Me service.
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Cheapest is often a reseller.**
	- Comparison sites list resellers alongside official stores.
	- Authenticity cannot be guaranteed from reseller listings.
	- For K-beauty, buy from the brand's own store or an authorised retailer.
	- Every K-beauty store in our [Where to Shop](https://koreahaul.com/where-to-shop) directory is official or authorised.
</callout>
- **Domestic shipping.** A cheaper item with ₩3,000 shipping is not cheaper. Many stores offer free domestic shipping (무료배송) above a spend threshold, so buying two items from one store can cost less than one item from the cheapest store.
- **Option surcharges.** The headline price is the cheapest option. Size, colour, or set upgrades add on at checkout.
- **Member-only prices.** Some stores show a lower price once logged in.
## Group by store
- One order from one store usually means one delivery and one domestic shipping charge.
- Order the same items separately and you get a delivery and a charge for each, even from the same store.
- Some stores split an order anyway, by warehouse or by supplier, so one order does not always mean one parcel.
- Put everything from one store in one Buy For Me request. That usually saves more than hunting the lowest unit price.
- The Buy For Me fee is charged per product, so five items cost five fees either way. Grouping saves on domestic delivery, not on our fee.
<callout color="gray_bg">
	**Three different albums at ₩18,000 each, from one store**
	- Ordered separately: ₩54,000 + ₩3,000 × 3 = **₩63,000**
	- One order: ₩54,000 + ₩3,000 = **₩57,000**
	- One order, above the store's ₩50,000 free-shipping threshold: **₩54,000**
	- Buy For Me fee either way: ₩2,000 × 3 = **₩6,000**
	Three copies of the same album would be one product, so **₩2,000**.
</callout>
