## Shipping notes
- **Light sticks** mostly ship without batteries. Where one is included, we may have to remove it.
	- Carriers that accept batteries cap it at 1 to 2 devices per shipment.
	- EMS Korea Post takes no batteries at all.
	- International Standard Shipping accepts them, except to Japan, China, and Hong Kong.
	- They classify as either a festive article or a portable lamp, which changes the duty rate by country.
- Albums are heavy. Ten albums can outweigh a coat.
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Brand collaborations cannot use International Standard Shipping.** Ordinary artist merchandise is fine, but anything co-branded with an international label - a Nike collaboration, for example - has to go by FedEx, EMS Korea Post, or SF Express.
</callout>
- **Posters** cost more to ship in a tube - carriers treat it as special packaging. We can pack the tube inside a regular box instead, which avoids the surcharge.
