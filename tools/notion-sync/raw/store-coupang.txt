## Good to know
- **Blue Rocket badge (로켓배송)** - sold and shipped by Coupang itself. The most reliable option, and usually the fastest to reach our warehouse.
- **Orange Seller Rocket badge (판매자로켓)** - a third-party seller using Coupang's delivery. Check the seller's reviews.
- Items without a badge are sold by marketplace sellers and can take longer to arrive.
