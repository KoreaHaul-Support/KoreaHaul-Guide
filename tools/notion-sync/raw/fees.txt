## Buy For Me service fee
Charged per product line, not per unit. Three copies of the same album count as one. **Premium is charged per unit.** Quantity limits apply to every Buy For Me request.
<callout icon="🏢" color="yellow_bg">
	**Launch promotion:** Basic and Standard are **₩0** for requests submitted by 31 December 2026. Premium is not included.
</callout>
<callout color="blue_bg">
	**Bulk, wholesale, and business orders** are priced separately. Email [business@koreahaul.com](mailto:business@koreahaul.com).
</callout>
### Basic Proxy - ₩2,000
- Simple online purchase
- Unboxing media included
### Standard Proxy - ₩5,000
- Everything in Basic
- Seller communication included
- Required for Karrot (Daangn), Bunjang, Joongonara, and similar second-hand marketplaces
### Premium Proxy - ₩30,000 per unit
- Offline pickup service
- Pop-up stores
- Retired LEGO sets
- High-value Pokémon cards
- Items priced over ₩500,000
For Premium offline pickup, contact us first to confirm availability before submitting your request.

## Storage
Free for 45 days from check-in. Each package has its own countdown.
- **After 45 days:** ₩1,000 per day per package. ₩2,000 per day if dimensional weight is 10 kg or more.
- **Maximum 90 days.** After 90 days with no activity we send a notice. No reply within 14 days and we may sell, donate, or dispose of the contents.
Dimensional weight: L × W × H (cm) ÷ 5000.
<callout icon="🧾" color="gray_bg">
	Storage is not billed separately. It appears as an Additional Charge on your shipping invoice.
</callout>
## International shipping
Priced by destination and chargeable weight, which is the actual or dimensional weight, whichever is higher.
See the Shipping Calculator and the [Shipping Policy](https://koreahaul.com/support/shipping-policy).
