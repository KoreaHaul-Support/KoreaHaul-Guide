Snacks are the most commonly refused category at customs, and food imports are prohibited outright in many countries. Check your country's rules before you submit a Buy For Me request, not after.
<callout icon="🚫" color="yellow_bg">
	Nothing fresh, frozen, or containing meat - including meat-based instant soups and most dried meat snacks. Many countries also block dairy and egg ingredients.
</callout>

## Usually fine
Instant noodles without meat sachets, biscuits, candy, chocolate, seaweed, dried fruit, tea, coffee sticks.
## Often restricted
Honey, kimchi and other fermented foods, red ginseng products, anything marketed as a health supplement.
## Country rules vary sharply
Australia, New Zealand, and Taiwan inspect food shipments closely. The EU restricts most animal-derived products outright. Check your country page.
A refused food item is destroyed, not returned, and the shipping already paid is not refunded.
## Practical notes
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Watch the shelf life.** Check the date on the original product listing or the product label before you order, and ship food promptly rather than holding it.
</callout>
- Snacks are bulky and light, so volumetric weight climbs fast.
- Crisps arrive crushed unless packed with care.
- Ramen packs sold in fives or tens are much cheaper per unit than singles.
