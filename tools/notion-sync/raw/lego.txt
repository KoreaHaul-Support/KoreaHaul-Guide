Retired LEGO sets are often cheaper in Korea than anywhere else.
Demand is lower for some themes, so sets that command a premium abroad sit at ordinary prices here.
Star Wars is the clearest example - it is far less popular in Korea, so retired sets can be a fraction of what they cost elsewhere.
This is why so many Korean eBay sellers deal in retired LEGO.
## Where to buy
- [**Coupang**](https://coupang.com) - usually cheapest on mainstream sets, frequent flash discounts.
- [**LEGO official store on Naver**](https://brand.naver.com/lego) - the brand's own Korean store.
- **Naver Smart Stores** - exclusives and older sets.
- [**Lotte On**](https://lotteon.com), [**SSG**](https://ssg.com) - during department store sale periods.
- **Bunjang, Joongonara** - retired sets. Condition claims are the seller's, not ours. Retired sets need Premium Proxy.
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**LEGO ships by FedEx only.** International Standard Shipping does not accept branded products, and LEGO is on that list.
</callout>
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Box condition is not guaranteed.** Retired sets have usually sat in storage for years. Getting one with sharp corners, no dents, and no damage is luck, not something we can promise or check for.
	If you need the box in perfect condition, we are not the right service. Box condition is never grounds for a return.
</callout>
## The problem with LEGO
<callout icon="📦" color="yellow_bg">
	LEGO boxes are mostly air. Carriers charge the higher of actual and dimensional weight, so a large set can cost more to ship than it cost to buy.
</callout>
**We do not remove LEGO boxes.** The set ships in its retail packaging and you pay for the volume.
- Large sets are expensive to ship. Check the Shipping Calculator before you buy, not after.
## Other notes
- Korean sets include Korean-language instructions. Build numbering is identical.
- Check the set number, not the name. Korean titles are translated and may be inconsistent.
