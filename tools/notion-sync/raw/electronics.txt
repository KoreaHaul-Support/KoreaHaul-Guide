<callout color="yellow_bg">

## Before you order
<callout icon="🔌" color="yellow_bg">
	Korea runs **220V at 60Hz** with Type C and F plugs. A Korean appliance on a 110V supply will not work, and an adapter does not change the voltage. Check the label says 100-240V before you buy.
</callout>
- **Batteries must be built into the device.** Loose batteries and power banks cannot be shipped at all.
	- Every carrier that accepts batteries caps it at 1 to 2 devices per shipment.
	- FedEx - accepted.
	- SF Express - accepted.
	- International Standard Shipping - accepted, except to Japan, China, and Hong Kong.
	- EMS Korea Post - not accepted.
	- Carrier policy can change without notice.
- Korean models often ship with Korean-only menus and no international warranty.
- Some devices are region-locked, including game consoles and streaming hardware.
## What we do not do
We document unboxing media. We do not power on, pair, activate, or test devices, and we do not check whether something works. If a device is faulty, that is between you and the seller, and the report window is 5 days from the unboxing media.
## Customs
Electronics attract higher scrutiny than most categories, and several countries require safety or radio certification for imported devices. Above your duty-free threshold, expect the box to be examined.
