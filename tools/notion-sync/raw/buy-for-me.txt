## How it works
1. Submit the product URL via a [custom request form](https://koreahaul.com/services/buy-for-me/create), or request directly from our [Bunjang](https://koreahaul.com/bunjang), [KH Picks](https://koreahaul.com/koreahaul-shop), and Popular products listings.
2. Select available options and quantity. Quantity limits may apply to certain product categories or stores.
3. Choose your purchase rules:
	- **Quantity rule** - exact quantity or cancel, or partial quantity allowed
	- **Price rule** - requested price or less, or allow up to an amount you set
	- **Domestic shipping** - accept if required, or cancel if required
	See [Setting Purchase Rules](https://app.notion.com/p/3e7dc07682c2815ea43cf62dc8b45803) for how each rule works.
4. Pay the item cost, domestic shipping, and Buy For Me fee upfront.
Once payment is received, we purchase the item on your behalf.

## Good to know
- Works with Naver Store, Coupang, Gmarket, Bunjang, Daangn, KREAM, brand sites, and most Korean shops.
- If the real price is lower than what you paid, we refund the difference as Store Credit.
- The Buy For Me fee covers our work on the purchase. It applies once we have reviewed your request, whether or not the purchase succeeds.
- If the seller cancels your order before shipment, a ₩1,000 handling fee applies.
- Buying generally takes 1-3 hours during business hours. Korean domestic delivery takes another 2 to 5 days.
- Send the full product link, not a search result, and copy the option text in Korean exactly as the listing shows it.
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Check your unboxing media within 5 days.** You are responsible for reviewing the unboxing media we provide. If you do not report a seller-related issue within 5 calendar days, the item is treated as accepted as received for Buy For Me purposes.
</callout>
