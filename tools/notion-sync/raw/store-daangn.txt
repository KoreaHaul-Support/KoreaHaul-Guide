## Buy For Me tier
- **Basic ₩2,000** - Safe Payment (안전거래) listings, bought outright through Daangn, the same way as Bunjang
- **Standard ₩5,000** - the seller agrees to ship, and we handle the conversation in Korean
- **Premium ₩30,000 per unit** - the seller only deals in person, so someone has to collect
<callout color="yellow_bg">
	**Safe Payment orders are final.** Once placed, they cannot be cancelled or returned, and no refund is possible. Check the listing carefully before you request.
</callout>
## Good to know
- Many Daangn sellers only trade in person within their neighbourhood. Contact us first so we can confirm we can reach the location.
- Listings go quickly. Send the link as soon as you find it.
- Condition and accuracy of the listing are the seller's responsibility.
- More tips on the <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dbdc07682c281579360dec86ce6abcb"/> page.
## Shipping notes
- If the country of origin cannot be identified, the item is declared as made in China.
- **United States:** second-hand items often come without CPSC certification. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dedc07682c281a0ae6bfc823fb0196d"/>.
