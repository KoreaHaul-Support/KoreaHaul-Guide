## Good to know
- **Digital coupon codes** need Premium Buy For Me (₩30,000 per code) and payment by Wise or Bank Transfer, because of the risk of seller fraud. Codes are sent only to your registered email.
- Items are sold by individuals, not by Bunjang. Condition and accuracy of the listing are the seller's responsibility.
- Check the photos and description carefully before you request.
- Box condition is never grounds for a return.
- More tips on the <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dbdc07682c281579360dec86ce6abcb"/> page.
## Shipping notes
- If the country of origin cannot be identified, the item is declared as made in China. This happens often with Bunjang items.
- **United States:** second-hand items often come without CPSC certification. Toys, plush, light sticks, and children's products may be held. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3dedc07682c281a0ae6bfc823fb0196d"/>.
- **Branded products (Nike, LEGO, Pokémon, and similar) cannot use International Standard Shipping.**
