Three steps from a Korean store to your door.
1. **Create your account.** You get a personal Postbox code, for example `KF2331`, and our warehouse address in Incheon. A ₩5,000 Welcome Coupon is issued when you sign up.
2. **Get your items to our warehouse.** We buy them for you with Buy For Me, or you shop yourself and ship to us with Receive For Me.
3. **Ship them home.** Combine everything into one box, choose a carrier, and pay.
## Your Postbox code
Your Postbox code is how we match a package to you. Two letters and four numbers, like `KF2331`. It appears on your dashboard the moment you sign up, and it never changes.
> Always include your Postbox code when you send something to our warehouse.
## Your Korean address
Use this exactly as written when a Korean store asks where to deliver. Put **your own name** as the recipient, not "KoreaHaul".
```javascript
Recipient: [Your name]
인천광역시 부평구 부평북로 58, 2층 201호 [Your Postbox code]
58, Bupyeongbuk-ro, Bupyeong-gu, Incheon
2F, Suite # 201 [Your Postbox code]
Postal code 21302
Republic of Korea
Phone: 010-5725-1222
```
Korean checkout forms usually want the Korean lines.
- **Address search (주소 찾기):** search for `인천광역시 부평구 부평북로 58`. The form fills in the postal code (우편번호) for you.
- **Address 2 (상세주소):** `2층 201호 [Your Postbox code]`
> Without a Postbox code we cannot match your package to your account, which causes delays. We hold it as an unidentified package and list it on our [Notice page](https://koreahaul.com/support/notice) - check there and claim it.
## What happens after your package arrives
- We usually check it in 1-2 days after it reaches the warehouse.
- We document unboxing media and upload it to your dashboard for you to review.
- It appears in your warehouse list with its check-in date.
- Your 45 free storage days start from that check-in date.
Items checked in on different days each have their own countdown.
## Ready to ship
- Request a shipment from [Ship For Me](https://koreahaul.com/account/ship-for-me).
- We pack, weigh, and measure the box.
- Pick a carrier from the real prices we quote.
- Check the Shipping Calculator on [KoreaHaul](https://koreahaul.com/) to estimate the cost before you request.
